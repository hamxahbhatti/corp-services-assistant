# Executive Compensation Bands 2026 (Confidential)

## 1. Scope
This document lists salary bands for grades 14 to 16. Access is limited to the HR Compensation team.

## 2. Bands
Grade 14 basic salary band: AED 58,000 to AED 66,000 per month. Grade 15: AED 67,000 to AED 78,000. Grade 16: AED 79,000 to AED 92,000. Annual leave for these grades follows the Annual Leave Policy.
