# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Pre-approval
All business travel must be approved by the line manager before booking.

## 2. Expense claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date with original receipts. Claims above AED 5,000 require director approval.

## 3. Per diem
The daily allowance for international travel is AED 750 for grades 1 to 9 and AED 1,000 for grade 10 and above.
