# Acceptable Use and Remote Access Policy

## 1. Remote access (VPN)
VPN access is granted only after line-manager approval and completion of the annual security awareness training. VPN access for vendor projects is time-bound and expires after 90 days.

## 2. Software
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Other software must be requested through the IT service catalogue and reviewed by IT Security.

## 3. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access.
