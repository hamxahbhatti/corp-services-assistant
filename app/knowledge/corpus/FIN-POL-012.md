# Payment Terms Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets payment terms for supplier invoices and the handling of blocked or overdue invoices.

## 2. Standard terms
Approved supplier invoices are paid within 30 calendar days of the invoice receipt date, unless the contract states otherwise.

## 3. Three-way match
Every invoice is matched against the purchase order and the goods receipt in SAP. An invoice that fails the match is blocked for payment.

## 4. Blocked and overdue invoices
### 4.1 Notification
The requester is notified automatically when an invoice linked to their purchase order is blocked.
### 4.2 Resolution of discrepancies
Price or quantity discrepancies must be resolved by the Accounts Payable team within 5 working days of the requester's confirmation. The requester should contact Accounts Payable with the invoice number, the purchase order number and the confirmed correct amount. Accounts Payable is reachable at accounts-payable@authority.example.
### 4.3 Escalation
If a discrepancy is not resolved within 10 working days, the requester may escalate to the Finance Operations manager.
