# How to Request VPN Access or New Software

## 1. VPN access
Open the IT service catalogue item "Remote Access (VPN)". Provide the business justification, the end date and your line manager. The request creates a ServiceNow RITM and is fulfilled within 2 working days after approval.

## 2. New software
Open the catalogue item "Software Request". Provide the software name, version and licence details. Standard catalogue software is installed within 3 working days.
