# Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines annual leave entitlement, accrual and carry-over rules for all employees of the Authority.

## 2. Entitlement
Employees in grades 1 to 9 are entitled to 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees in grade 10 and above are entitled to 30 working days. Leave accrues monthly from the first day of service.

## 3. Requesting leave
Annual leave must be requested through the HR self-service portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Requests of 5 days or fewer may be submitted 3 working days in advance. The line manager must approve the request before the leave starts.

## 4. Carry-over
Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March. Days above this limit lapse unless the Director of HR approves an exception in writing.

## 5. Public holidays
Official public holidays announced by the government do not count against annual leave.
