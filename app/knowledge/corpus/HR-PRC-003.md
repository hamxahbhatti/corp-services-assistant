# Leave Request Procedure

## 1. Check your balance
Open the HR self-service portal and select "My Leave Balance". The balance shows accrued, taken and planned days.

## 2. Submit the request
Choose the leave type, dates and a delegate who will cover urgent work. Attach supporting documents for special leave types such as sick leave above two days.

## 3. Approval
The request is routed to your line manager. Requests longer than 15 working days also require approval from the department director.

## 4. Changes and cancellation
You can cancel or change an approved request before the leave starts. After the start date, contact HR Operations.
