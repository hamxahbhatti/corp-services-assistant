# Procurement Thresholds Policy

## 1. Purchase request
Every purchase starts with a purchase request (PR) in SAP. The PR must include the cost centre, a description, the estimated value and the budget line.

## 2. Quotation thresholds
Purchases below AED 10,000 require one quotation. Purchases from AED 10,000 to AED 50,000 require three written quotations. Purchases above AED 50,000 require a formal tender through the Procurement Department.

## 3. Approval matrix
PRs up to AED 50,000 are approved by the department director. PRs above AED 50,000 also require approval from the Procurement Committee. IT hardware and software purchases must be reviewed by the IT department before approval.
