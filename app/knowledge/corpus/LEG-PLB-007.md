# Vendor Dispute Playbook (Legal only)

## 1. Invoice disputes with suppliers
When a supplier disputes a blocked invoice or a price variance, Legal Affairs may withhold payment and apply contractual penalties of up to 10 percent. Do not share the negotiation position with the requester or the supplier.

## 2. Gulf Supplies LLC
Gulf Supplies LLC is under contract review. Any price variance above AED 2,000 must be escalated to Legal Affairs before payment is released.
